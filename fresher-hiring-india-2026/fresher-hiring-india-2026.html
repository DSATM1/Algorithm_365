The five structural facts

  1. Batch hiring is dead. HCLTech Group CHRO (Jul 2026): hiring now follows "quarterly demand patterns rather than fixed annual targets." TCS made ~25,000 FY27 fresher offers — lowest since FY20, down from ~44,000.
  2. AI absorbed the classic fresher workload. NASSCOM's own estimate: AI can perform 20–40% of common tech tasks — routine coding, test-case generation, documentation. That was the Indian IT fresher job description.
  3. Pay went bimodal inside the same company. TCS pays ₹3.4 LPA (Ninja) and ₹11.8 LPA (Prime) off the same test. Infosys pays ₹3.6 LPA (SE) and ₹21 LPA (Specialist Programmer L3). The 6× spread is decided by 60–90 minutes of coding.
  4. GCCs are the growth engine, Bangalore is the capital. 2,117 GCCs / 2.36m people / $98.4bn (Zinnov–NASSCOM). Karnataka's GCC Policy targets 1,000 GCCs, 3.5 lakh jobs and 1 lakh subsidised internships by 2029.
  5. Evidence replaced credentials. Employers "prioritised internship experience, GitHub portfolios and cloud certifications over academic credentials alone" (HireDoor, 18,400 listings). 30% of Indian employers now hire without degree requirements vs 19% globally (WEF).

The "stale batch" problem — what's true and what's folklore

Folklore: "2025 passouts are locked out."
Reality: TCS NQT 2026 explicitly accepts 2024, 2025 and 2026 batches. Amazon's new-grad template says "graduated within the last 24 months." Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Tech Mahindra, DXC and Oracle all list multiple batches.
The three real constraints — none is the year on your degree:
  1. 24-month academic gap cap + "no extended education." A backlog-delayed degree disqualifies you at TCS regardless of batch.
  2. Age bands. 18–28 at TCS/TechM/Wipro; 18–30 at Cognizant, "no relaxations."
  3. Recruiter inference about the blank year. Indian unicorns accept 2024/2025 batches "if you have either a freelance portfolio or one prior internship that ended on good terms." A 12-month blank is screened out; a 12-month gap with GitHub / freelance / internship evidence is not.

Context for why employers are wary: median job tenure for Indian workers aged 15–29 is 13 months, and replacement hiring is ~40% of Gen Z hiring demand (Quess, Aug 2026). Off-campus also carries a salary penalty — comparable firms offer ₹5–6 LPA on-campus vs ₹2.5–3 LPA off-campus.

Offer-to-joining is now endemic

Post-offer waits went 4–8 weeks (2021–22) → 2–4 months (2023–24) → 6–18 months for parts of the 2024–26 cohorts. NITES filed Labour Ministry complaints over TCS delaying 600+ lateral hires and Wipro delaying 200+ candidates. Tech Mahindra reportedly had ~1,000 graduates still awaiting offer letters mid-2026. Oracle withdrew campus offers. Infosys terminated 300+ freshers at Mysuru in Feb 2025 for failing internal training assessments.

Practical rule: no joining date within 90 days of the offer letter (or 180 days of selection-only) → treat it as a signal, run a parallel search, document every communication, escalate via the placement cell or NITES.

The machine coding round — Bangalore's signature format

90–150 minutes, your own IDE, working runnable code required — not pseudocode, not a class diagram. A compact real-world spec, shipped as an in-memory application with a driver class. No DB, no framework, no UI. Pure OOP.

Canonical problems: parking lot · Splitwise · vending machine · snake & ladder · elevator bank · cab booking · tic-tac-toe · chess move validator · library management · in-memory key-value store · rate limiter · LRU cache · Trello · 2048

Scoring rubric

Code design & class structure~30%
It compiles and runs correctly~25%
SOLID + design patterns~20%
Extensibility — they add a requirement mid-review~15%
Edge cases & validation~10%

Time split: 10–15 min design on paper → 15–20 min models → 25–30 min business logic → 10 min driver → 5–10 min polish. Directory hygiene (models/, services/, strategies/, exceptions/) is itself scored.

Who runs it: Flipkart (originator), Swiggy, Uber India, Ola, Udaan, Gojek, CRED, Razorpay, PhonePe, Meesho, Zepto. CRED's is the most demanding — a 2.5-hour LLD elimination gate preceded by a 24-hour take-home and a DSA round in a Google Doc, not an IDE.

This single skill separates candidates who clear Bangalore product interviews from those who don't — and almost nobody prepares for it. A beautiful design that doesn't compile scores near zero.

DSA topic frequency for Indian fresher rounds

Very high — arrays, strings, hashing, two pointers, sliding window, binary search
High — trees/BST, recursion & backtracking, stacks & queues, sorting, linked lists
Medium-high — graphs (BFS/DFS, grids, topo sort), heaps/top-k, greedy
Medium — DP (knapsack, coin change, LIS, grid DP)
Low — tries, bit manipulation, segment trees

Target: solve a LeetCode-medium in under 30 minutes; recognise the pattern within ~2 minutes of reading. Practice split commonly recommended: 150 problems — 30 easy / 100 medium / 20 hard.

Company skews: Amazon — graph traversal + sliding window (~60% of rounds). Google — DP + greedy, "can you do better?", brute force won't clear. Microsoft — trees dominate (~80% of reported interviews). Flipkart/Swiggy — arrays dressed as e-commerce scenarios, often SQL + DSA together. Razorpay/PhonePe — strings, edge cases, financial precision.

Complexity discussion is not optional. State time and space before coding. OA constraints are an encoded hint — n ≤ 10⁵ means O(n log n) at worst.

Company exam patterns worth memorising

Amazon OA

2–3.5 hrs: 2 DSA (medium–hard), 70–105 min + Work Styles Survey ~80 Likert items mapped to the 16 LPs (paired statements detect contradictions — a bad survey can sink good coding) + sometimes debugging (6–8 snippets). Failure = silent rejection + 6–12 month cooling-off. Loop ends with the Bar Raiser, an external interviewer with veto power.

Google 2026

Restructured loop piloted this year: a Code Comprehension round (read, debug, optimise existing code), a revamped Googleyness & Leadership round with technical design, and an open-ended engineering round for junior candidates replacing one classic round.

Goldman Sachs

HackerRank OA 90–120 min → HireVue pre-recorded → 2 CoderPad live rounds → Superday: 3–5 back-to-back. Mix ~15% easy / 60% medium / 25% hard, with ~20% of interview time on puzzles. ~2-min answer caps make AI assistance impractical.

JPMorgan Chase

Code for Good is the main fresher door — a 24-hour hackathon in teams of 5–6 for real NGOs. ~2,000–3,000 selected → 300–500 offers. Intern stipend ₹80,000/month.

Zoho

Five rounds: quant (1.5 hr) → programming in C (75 min) → advanced programming → technical (OOP/DBMS/OS) → HR. No minimum CGPA. Plus an offline LLD round: build a console game with collision detection and difficulty levels.

Juspay

~30-min quiz → 90-min OA → 2-day hackathon building a real app. Functional programming required. Any B.Tech discipline, no backlogs. 4–6 week cycle. ₹19–27 LPA.

CS fundamentals — the services hit-list

OOPs — four pillars · overloading vs overriding · abstract class vs interface · runtime polymorphism

DBMS — normalisation to 3NF · ACID · all JOIN types with examples · indexing · views · SQL ("second-highest salary" is near-universal, plus GROUP BY/HAVING, self-joins, duplicate removal)

OS — deadlock and the four Coffman conditions · CPU scheduling · paging vs segmentation · process vs thread · virtual memory

CN — TCP vs UDP · OSI layers · "what happens when you type a URL" · HTTP vs HTTPS · DNS

Pattern is broad and fast — one or two questions per subject, definition then a "why." Service companies weight these heavily; product companies sample them briefly.

~38% of interviews are being rebuilt in response to AI flagging — expect more in-person finals, live debugging and project deep-dives replacing take-homes. Ask the recruiter in writing what the AI policy is for your specific loop.

AI interviewers are a real hazard too. 63% of job seekers globally have faced one; 38% withdrew from a process requiring one; 51% who completed one were ghosted (Fortune, May 2026). Indian developers flagged Micro1 in May 2026 for 50–60 min fully-AI one-way interviews followed by generic rejections within 24 hours, with allegations of talent-database harvesting rather than real openings. Proctoring false-positive rates disproportionately hit darker-skinned candidates and poor home environments — if auto-flagged, request human review in writing.

Project deep-dive: how they detect fake projects

Expect 10–20 minutes on your main project: architecture, tech-stack justification ("why MongoDB and not Postgres?"), your specific contribution, hardest bug, what you'd change, how it scales, how you tested and deployed it.

Detection is by depth-probing: why this library or data structure · walk through this specific file · what are the failure modes · modify this feature live · cross-check the commit history. HackerEarth names the tell for AI-generated OA code — "a clean graph-traversal implementation the candidate cannot explain in the follow-up" — and recommends a 10-minute live explanation round after every OA. Vendors report roughly half of job seekers admit to using GenAI to misrepresent skills.

System design for freshers — yes, but basic

In India, LLD is far more common than HLD for SDE-1. Fresher-appropriate prompts: TinyURL, parking lot, rate limiter, elevator, basic Instagram feed, a coupon service with schema — not "design Netflix." Microsoft's explicit bar: "break the problem into components, pick an appropriate data store, explain one trade-off." Basic API design (REST resources, status codes, idempotency, pagination) recurs in fintech.

The two numbers most students get wrong

1. CTC is not take-home. Fixed pay is typically 60–70% of CTC (IT services better, 85–90%). A ₹10 LPA CTC yields roughly ₹55,000–60,000/month in hand. Amazon's stock is backloaded 5% / 15% / 40% / 40% — year-1 cash is far below the headline. At private companies, ESOPs are illiquid (Flipkart, CRED, Razorpay, Meesho); Zomato/Eternal and Paytm equity is exchange-traded. Evaluate base + guaranteed bonus as the real number.
2. ₹6 LPA — not ₹4 LPA — is the Bangalore viability line.

Scam patterns — freshers are the primary target

  • Any request for money is a scam, full stop. TCS: "TCS does not ask for any sort of payment or security deposit from candidates at any stage." Reported fees: ₹3,000–₹25,000 typically, escalating to ₹1,00,000.
  • Impersonation targets: TCS, Infosys, Accenture, Wipro, HCL, Tech Mahindra, Cognizant, Capgemini, IBM. Verbatim live scam: "This is HR Anjali from Infosys Pvt Ltd, Bangalore… Package: 6.5 LPA + Bonus."
  • Channel: Telegram, WhatsApp, Gmail/Rediff/Yahoo instead of @company.com.
  • Documents: typos in the company name ("Tata Consultance Services"), bad logos, payment to a personal UPI ID.
  • Equipment scam: a Bengaluru case issued 40 fake appointment letters for a Manyata Tech Park firm, demanding ₹1.5 lakh each "for laptops."
  • No technical interview: a documented case — one HR call, salary discussion, direct ₹33 LPA offer, 2-year bond, no skills test. An offer with no technical round is a red flag, not a compliment.
  • Legitimate but adverse: unpaid "training periods," training bonds, and colleges/consultancies charging a % of CTC post-placement (one Bengaluru college reportedly demanded 21%).

The 90-day plan

Days 1–30 · Fix the filters that eliminate you before a human reads anything

  1. Establish the record. Your exact 10th / 12th / graduation percentages, your cumulative academic gap in months, your age. These four numbers decide which half of the market is even open. Below 60% or with an active backlog, your first move is clearing the backlog — not applying.
  2. Build one deployable project with a real README — problem statement, architecture, tech-stack justification, live link, honest limitations. This is now screened ahead of CGPA. If you've written under 3,000 lines of code, that's the first gap (a real Bangalore JD says so verbatim).
  3. Start the DSA clock. 150 problems: 30 easy / 100 medium / 20 hard. Sequence: arrays → strings → hashing → two pointers → sliding window → binary search → stacks/queues → linked lists → trees/BST → graphs → heaps → greedy → DP.
  4. Register for the mass funnels now — they have fixed windows: TCS NextStep, Infosys portal + InfyTQ, Wipro careers/elite, Cognizant GenC, Accenture indiacampus, Capgemini via Superset.

Days 31–60 · Build the differentiators

  1. AWS Solutions Architect – Associate. Highest evidence-to-effort credential in the Indian market.
  2. Learn the machine coding round. Solve 8–10 canonical LLD problems end-to-end in 90 minutes each. This is the single highest-leverage, least-prepared-for skill in Bangalore product hiring.
  3. Fix the communication filter. Cognizant SVAR, Capgemini spoken English and Accenture's 63-question AI-graded assessment eliminate technically-competent candidates every cycle. Practise reading aloud, sentence repetition, 30-second structured answers, and two essays under a 20-minute clock.
  4. Add one real AI project — a RAG pipeline or agent over data you care about, with an evaluation section — and be able to explain every design choice. 60–70% of fresher JDs now name AI/ML; Razorpay literally evaluates "AI Edge."

Days 61–90 · Apply with the right channel mix

  1. Channel allocation: ~40% company career portals and Greenhouse/Lever boards (highest signal) · 20% Cutshort / Instahyre / Unstop · 20% referrals via LinkedIn outreach to engineers, not recruiters · 10% Naukri with 0–1 yr + 1-day-freshness · 10% hackathons you're still eligible for.
  2. Target September–November 2026. The year's second hiring peak. October has one of the highest off-campus callback rates because competitors are absorbed in campus season. December is dead. First peak is January–March.
  3. Prepare per-segment, not generically. Services → aptitude + pseudocode + communication + CS fundamentals. GCC/product → DSA depth + behavioural STAR + basic LLD. Startups → machine coding + project depth + ownership stories.
  4. Ask every recruiter three questions, in writing: What's the AI-tool policy for my loop? What's the expected date of joining? Is there a service agreement, and what's its value and duration?

Ten things to verify before accepting anything

  1. Is the offer letter from an @company.com domain?
  2. Has any money been requested? (If yes: scam. No exceptions.)
  3. Is there a date of joining in writing, within 90 days?
  4. What is the bond, in months and rupees? (Wipro Elite: 12 months / ₹75,000. Wipro WILP: 60 months. Capgemini: ~2 years.)
  5. What is the fixed component vs variable, and the actual monthly in-hand?
  6. If ESOPs are quoted: liquid (listed) or illiquid (private)? Strike price and vesting cliff?
  7. Is the work location guaranteed or "any company location"? (Mphasis, Cognizant, TCS reserve allocation rights.)
  8. Are rotational or night shifts required? (DXC, Tech Mahindra, Infosys BPM, Cognizant.)
  9. Is there a post-joining training assessment you can be terminated for failing? (Infosys terminated 300+ freshers at Mysuru for exactly this.)
  10. Did you have a real technical interview?

What this research could not establish

  • Mid-tier firms publish almost nothing. Coforge, Nagarro, Birlasoft, Zensar, Sonata, Happiest Minds, Cyient, KPIT, Quest Global, UST, Virtusa, Atos, Tata Elxsi run opportunistic postings, not branded programs. Any "cutoff" quoted for them online is inference.
  • CTC is unpublished for most employers — IBM, Deloitte USI, Genpact, Qualcomm, TI, NVIDIA, Walmart, Target, Amex, ServiceNow, Wells Fargo, Morgan Stanley. Every circulating number is candidate-reported.
  • ~35 named companies could not be verified within the research window (Intel, Micron, AMD, Arista, Juniper, VMware, Dell, HPE, Atlassian, Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Apple India, LinkedIn India; Hasura, MoEngage, Whatfix, Darwinbox, Zeta, Setu, Chargebee, Postman, BrowserStack, Freshworks India, Nykaa, Lenskart, Paytm, Rapido, Delhivery, Udaan and others). Absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.
  • Employability figures disagree by 14 points — India Skills Report says 56.35% national / 80% CS; Mercer|Mettl says 42.6%. The optimistic number measures test-passing; the pessimistic one measures job-readiness. Plan against the lower figure.
  • Eligibility changes every cycle. Re-verify every figure on the live requisition — batch years, CGPA gates and locations change per posting.
